(About) Behind the frame

Hi, I’m
Arshad.

Motion designer and creative director from Kerala. I make brands move — and I care about every single frame of it.

Arshad — Kerala, INHover to look behind the frame

I started with a camera and a timeline. Five years later, I design, animate, shoot, render, edit and colour — all under one roof.

Most projects lose something every time they change hands: the idea, the feeling, the detail. I work differently. I take a project from the first sketch to the final export myself, so what we agree on is exactly what you get.

Over the years I’ve made 160+ pieces for 40+ brands — festive campaigns for LuLu, CGI billboards for Hive and AIBI, explainers for startups, logo animations for new brands and AI films for healthcare. Different crafts, one point of view: motion should make people feel something and remember who made them feel it.

Based in Kerala, working with brands across India, the UAE and beyond.
